Opportunity and Bid form flow

OPPORTUNITY FORM

One parent Opportunity is OPEN TO BIDDING

Multiple Bid records may be created against it.

BID FORM

Draft Bid

The user may create and edit it. Draft Bids are excluded from scheduled aggregates and automatic classification.

WF ACTION

Submit Bid

Draft → Submitted, followed by an immediate SQMS parent-aggregate refresh.

SUBMITTED-ONLY DATA RULE

Calculate parent Opportunity statistics

Bid count, minimum/maximum purchase amount and lowest/highest fair-value percentage use only SUBMITTED Bids.

All Draft, advanced and final Bid statuses are excluded and remain unchanged.

  1. Opportunities closed during the run

Recipients come from centralized SMA To/Cc configuration. Bcc is out of scope.

Failure and recovery rules

Parent close fails

The Opportunity remains open, no Bids are classified, and the parent is retried later.

One child classification fails

The parent remains closed. Successful Bid actions remain complete. Only Bids still in SUBMITTED are retried.

What is generic and what is custom?

OwnerResponsibilitiesEmail behavior
SQMS UI/Application Opportunity/Bid business data, parent-child relationships, validation, manual decisions, record-specific recipient resolution and Opportunity-person updates on Bids. Supplies recipients and template data; never sends SMTP through workflow integration.
Custom SQMS Coordinator 7:30 reconciliation, 8:00 pickup/notices/reminders/close, quarter/year grouping, submitted-only filtering, pricing classification and recovery. Starts deterministic SqmsNotificationBatch workflows; does not call SMTP directly.
Generic Workflow Engine SqmsOpportunity, SqmsBid and SqmsNotificationBatch metadata; transitions, task/SLA, audit, idempotency and concurrency. Existing templates, recipient snapshots, outbox processing, retry and SMTP sender.
Backward-compatibility boundary: this design does not require changes to the generic outbox schema, sender, public workflow request contracts, transition engine, recipient contract or single-active-task model.

Deliberately unresolved items

Coordinator pickup-date persistence

The first 8:00 AM pickup date must be stored once and reused for reminders, close timing and retry. The physical storage location has not yet been selected.

Seller-task completion

The rule for closing the single Opportunity-level Seller task is out of scope until SQMS users confirm the desired business behavior.
